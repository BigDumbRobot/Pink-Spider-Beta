### New in beta 4

- **Pink Spider-DMP** now shows its own name, **PINK SPIDER-DMP**, at the top of the window, so you can tell it apart from Pink Spider at a glance.
- Pink Spider and Pink Spider-DMP are now built on the latest version of JUCE (9.0.5), the audio framework underneath them. The sound and the controls are unchanged.

It's a free update, so just install over beta 2.
