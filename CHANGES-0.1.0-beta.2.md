### New in beta 2: Pink Spider-DMP

A second plugin, **Pink Spider-DMP** (digital mic pre): the same channel with the Spider mic pre's real **6 / 21 / 36 / 51 / 66 dB** of gain, for a mic plugged into a line-level input. It's a separate download and installs alongside Pink Spider; Pink Spider itself is unchanged.

Digital gain after a line-level converter raises its noise too, so it's best for close-miked, loud sources. Set your interface input to its most sensitive level.
